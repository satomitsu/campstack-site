Frequently asked questions

My purchased plan doesn't show
Open Settings > Compare Plans and tap “Restore Purchases”. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you bought it with.
How do I cancel?
In the iPhone's Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. You keep the plan until the end of the period.
How do I stop AI analysis of photos?
Turn off “Analyze photos with AI” in Settings > Autofill from Photo. Estimates are then made on your iPhone only.
I can't see a shared plan
The owner may have stopped sharing, or you may have been removed from the plan. Offline, the last loaded contents are shown.
How do I move to a new iPhone?
Sign in on your current iPhone in Settings > Account, and your gear, sets and plans are backed up automatically. Sign in to the same account on the new iPhone, and when asked whether to restore, tap “Restore to This iPhone”. Shared plans come back when you sign in, too.
How do I delete my account?
In Settings > Account > Delete Account. Gear on your device and plans you haven't shared stay.
Can it tell sizes or weights from a photo?
No. Sizes and weights are never guessed from photos; enter only the values you know.
